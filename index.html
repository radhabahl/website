Through my workbook, accessories label, documentary, and gallery work, I'm building the case that heritage art isn't just worth saving — it's worth investing in.

02 · Enterprise

Hasta Kalā हस्तकला

An accessories label that turns end-of-roll textiles into hand-finished pieces carrying Indian folk art traditions forward.

Motifs from my research into Warli, Madhubani, Gond, Tanjore, Phad, Rangoli, Rogan and block printing move from the archive into everyday objects — laptop bags, totes, pouches and folders — finished by hand by women from low-income households working from home.

03 · Film

Inheritance

A documentary by Radha Bahl

What keeps a tradition alive? A film on Kangra miniature painting, the valley that shaped it, and the artists who inherit it today.

From the Himalayan foothills of Himachal Pradesh to the quiet discipline of the studio, Inheritance follows master painters, teachers and a new generation learning the craft — among them artists training through Chinmaya CORD.
